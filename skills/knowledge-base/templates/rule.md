# Member discount

- [rule] A member pays 10 less on an order of 100 or more, before tax
- [example] Member, order 100.00: pays 90.00 plus tax on 90.00
- [exception] Not combined with a promotion code; the larger discount wins
- [source] Pricing policy v3, product owner for checkout
